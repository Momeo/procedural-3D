procedural-3D

Zero-asset, spec-driven procedural monsters & living weapons for three.js. 35 species + 5 gun pals — geometry, textures and animation are all generated in code.

Single viewer One monster up close: species switcher, attack/stagger triggers, hit-volume overlay. Lineup All 35 species walking side by side — the silhouette readability check. Horde Hundreds of instanced monsters at a fixed draw-call budget; LOD + grid culling toggles. Shooter FPS hitscan playground: per-part OBB hit volumes, dismemberment, Verlet ragdolls. Gun pals Five living creature-weapons: gallery + first-person hold, idle/aim/fire/overheat states.
Serve the repo root over HTTP (e.g. python -m http.server) and open examples/. three.js r170 is vendored in examples/vendor/ — no network needed.
